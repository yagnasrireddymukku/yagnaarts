What happens after you submit?

  • We reply within 1 business day to confirm we've got your request.
  • You'll receive a digital proof within 2–3 days — nothing is crafted before you approve it.
  • Free revisions on the proof until you're happy with the design.
  • Once approved, most pieces ship within 5–7 working days.
What if I need it by a specific date?

Mention your deadline in the form — we'll confirm upfront whether we can meet it before you commit.

How many revisions are included?

Unlimited revisions to the digital proof — we don't move to production until you're fully happy.

Do you offer bulk pricing?

Yes — mention your quantity and we'll include bulk pricing in our reply. For weddings/corporate orders, see our dedicated gifting pages.
