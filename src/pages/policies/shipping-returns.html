Shipping & Returns

Last updated: January 2026

Shipping

We currently ship across India via tracked courier partners. A tracking link is shared by email/WhatsApp once your order is dispatched.

  • Ready-to-ship pieces dispatch within 2–4 working days of order confirmation.
  • Made-to-order pieces dispatch within the production window shown on the product page — this window starts only after you've approved your digital proof, for personalized items.
  • Shipping is a flat ₹99 per order, free on orders over ₹2,500.
  • International shipping for NRI gifting is on our near-term roadmap but not yet available.

Returns & Exchanges

Because most YagnaArts pieces are made specifically for you, our return policy works differently for personalized versus ready-to-ship items:

  • Personalized / made-to-order pieces cannot be returned for a change of mind, since they're crafted to your approved proof. If a piece arrives damaged, or doesn't match the proof you approved, we'll remake it or refund it in full — contact us within 48 hours of delivery with photos.
  • Ready-to-ship, non-personalized pieces can be returned within 7 days of delivery if unused and in original packaging. Reach out via our Contact page to start a return.
  • Refunds are issued to the original payment method within 5–7 working days of the returned item reaching us.

Damaged in Transit

Resin is durable but not unbreakable. If your order arrives damaged, please don't discard the packaging — photograph the item and packaging and contact us within 48 hours so we can arrange a replacement.
