Care Instructions

Resin is durable and long-lasting when cared for correctly. Every product page includes care notes specific to that piece — the guidance below covers the general rules that apply across our catalog.

General Care

  • Wipe with a soft, dry microfiber cloth. Avoid paper towels or abrasive sponges, which can dull the glossy finish over time.
  • Avoid prolonged direct sunlight — UV exposure can gradually yellow clear resin and fade preserved botanicals or photos over months/years.
  • Avoid harsh chemical cleaners, alcohol-based sprays, and nail polish remover — these can cloud or craze the resin surface.
  • Resin is heat-sensitive above ~50°C — keep pieces away from direct stovetop heat, car dashboards in peak sun, and heating vents.

Jewelry & Keychains

Avoid contact with perfume, water, and sweat where possible, and store in the pouch provided when not in use. Surgical-steel fittings are hypoallergenic but the resin itself is not waterproof for prolonged wear.

Home Decor & Wall Panels

Dust with a dry, soft brush rather than a wet cloth for textured or botanical pieces. Wall-mounted panels should use the hanging hardware provided — resin is heavier than it looks for its size.

Clocks & Mechanisms

Replace batteries only via the rear access panel. Never immerse the piece in water to clean the clock face.

Coasters & Trays

Safe for everyday use with room-temperature drinks and food. Avoid placing directly under extremely hot cookware, and dry promptly after wiping to avoid water spots.
