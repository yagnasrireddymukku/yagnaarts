Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 2026

What We Collect

When you place an order, submit a custom request, or contact us, we collect the information you provide directly: name, email, phone number, shipping address, and any photos or details submitted for personalization. We also collect standard analytics data (pages visited, device type, approximate location) via Google Analytics.

How We Use It

  • To fulfil and communicate about your order, including proofs and shipping updates.
  • To respond to enquiries submitted via our contact and custom-order forms.
  • To send occasional newsletter emails, only if you've subscribed — you can unsubscribe at any time.
  • To understand how visitors use our site, so we can improve it (via Google Analytics).

Third-Party Services

We use a small number of trusted third-party services to run the site and studio:

  • Google Sheets / Google Apps Script — to log contact and custom-order form submissions.
  • EmailJS — to send email notifications when a form is submitted.
  • WhatsApp — for direct order confirmation and support conversations you initiate.
  • Google Analytics — for anonymized traffic and usage analytics.

We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

Photos You Upload

Photos submitted for personalized pieces (e.g. a couple's photo, a pet photo, a baby's handprint) are used solely to create your piece and are not published or shared without your explicit permission.

Data Retention

We retain order and contact information as long as needed to fulfil orders and comply with basic accounting/tax record-keeping requirements, after which it's deleted on request.

Your Rights

You can request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data at any time by contacting us at hello@yagnaarts.com.
