Our Story

Yagna Sri's Studio

"I didn't want to sell resin art. I wanted to sell the feeling of opening a box and seeing a memory you didn't expect to see again."
— Yagna Sri, Founder

YagnaArts started the way most honest crafts do — small batches, poured on a kitchen table, sold one conversation at a time. What kept customers coming back wasn't just the resin work itself, but the care taken to get every personalized detail right before anything was ever cast.

Today the studio serves weddings, corporate gifting programs, and homes across India — but the founding principle hasn't changed: every personalized piece is proofed and approved by the customer before it's made, because a memory deserves to be gotten right, not guessed at.
